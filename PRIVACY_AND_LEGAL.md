# Privacy policy, terms and help: what to publish, and when to change them

The app's Privacy policy, Terms of use and Help page must always describe what the app **really** does. This file says where they live, what must be filled in before the app is published, and which code changes require a policy change.

**The rule:** a change that collects new data, uses data in a new way, asks for a new phone permission, or sends data to a new company is not finished until the Privacy policy says so. The tests below catch most of these automatically.

## 1. Where the documents live

There is **one copy** of each document. The app shows it, and the website publishes the same file.

| Document | Source file | In the app | Public web address |
|---|---|---|---|
| Privacy policy | `mobile/assets/legal/privacy_policy.md` | Profile › Help and legal | `https://zuno-infinite-service.vercel.app/privacy.html` |
| Terms of use | `mobile/assets/legal/terms_of_use.md` | Profile › Help and legal | `https://zuno-infinite-service.vercel.app/terms.html` |
| Help and support | `mobile/assets/legal/help.md` | Profile › Help and legal | `https://zuno-infinite-service.vercel.app/help.html` |
| Open-source licences | Generated by Flutter | Profile › Help and legal | — |
| Owner's details | `mobile/assets/legal/legal.json` | Filled into all three | Filled into all three |

- Every profile shows these links: customer (Profile tab), business (Profile tab) and worker (Profile screen). The shared list is `mobile/lib/shared/widgets/help_and_legal_links.dart`.
- The app draws the Markdown with `mobile/lib/shared/legal/legal_document.dart`. The web pages (`mobile/web/privacy.html`, `terms.html`, `help.html`, with `legal.js`) draw the same file in the browser.
- Only these Markdown forms are supported: `#`, `##`, `###` headings, `- ` bullets, paragraphs and `**bold**`. Keep to them.
- Write `{{fieldName}}` where an owner's detail goes, for example `{{contactEmail}}`. The value comes from `legal.json`. A blank value shows as "(to be added)".

## 2. Before publishing to the Play Store or App Store

These must all be done. An app store can reject the app, or remove it later, if any are missing.

1. **Fill in `mobile/assets/legal/legal.json`:**
   - `companyName`: the legal name of the company or person that runs Zuno.
   - `contactEmail`: a support email someone reads.
   - `grievanceOfficer`: the name of the Grievance Officer. Indian law (IT Rules 2011, DPDP Act 2023) requires one.
   - `postalAddress`: a postal address.
   - `lastUpdated`: the date the policy takes effect.
2. **Have a lawyer review** the three documents. They are written to match the app, but they are not legal advice.
3. **Deploy the web app** so the public addresses work. Open `privacy.html` on a phone and check it.
4. **Play Console › App content › Privacy policy:** enter `https://zuno-infinite-service.vercel.app/privacy.html`.
5. **Play Console › App content › Data safety:** answer it using section 4 of this file. It must match the policy.
6. **Account deletion (blocking):** Google Play requires apps with accounts to let people delete their account **inside the app**, and through a web page. The app does not have this yet. The policy says "write to us" for now. Build it before publishing (see section 5).
7. **Password reset (blocking for users):** the server does not send emails yet (`EmailService` only logs), so "forgot password" cannot work. Help says "write to us" for now. Connect an email provider before publishing.

## 3. When the code changes, change the policy

When your change does anything in the left column, update the policy section on the right **in the same change**, and set a new `lastUpdated` date in `legal.json`.

| If your change… | Update in `privacy_policy.md` | Also update |
|---|---|---|
| Stores a new field about a person (a new profile field, document, ID number) | 1. What we collect | Data safety form |
| Asks for a new phone permission (`AndroidManifest.xml`, iOS `Info.plist`) | 1. What we collect › From your phone | Data safety form, Help › Permissions |
| Adds a package that sends data off the phone (push, analytics, crash reports, maps, login with Google) | 3. Who can see your information | Data safety form |
| Adds a new company that receives data (payment, SMS, email, storage, hosting) | 3. Who can see your information | Data safety form |
| Shows a person's data to other users in a new place (public profile, new list) | 3. Who can see your information | — |
| Changes how long something is kept, or when it is deleted | 4. How long we keep it | — |
| Adds account deletion, data export or a new privacy setting | 5. Your choices and rights | Help |
| Starts sending email or SMS | 1 and 3 | Help |
| Changes booking, payment, advance, cancellation or refund rules | — | `terms_of_use.md` 6, `help.md` |
| Changes who can sign up (age, roles) | 7. Children | `terms_of_use.md` 2 |

If the change is significant (new kinds of data or new sharing), also tell users in the app.

### What the tests check for you

`mobile/test/legal/legal_documents_test.dart` fails when:

- `AndroidManifest.xml` asks for a permission the policy does not explain;
- `pubspec.yaml` gains a package that sends data off the phone (Firebase, analytics, crash reporting, Google sign-in, maps, payments) and the policy does not name the company;
- a document uses a `{{field}}` that `legal.json` does not have;
- a document uses Markdown the app cannot draw.

The test cannot see new database fields or new sharing rules. Those depend on the table above and on code review.

Recheck this table whenever section 3 above applies.

## 5. Known gaps to close

- **In-app account deletion**, plus a public deletion request page. Google Play requires both.
- **Email provider** for password reset and contact.
- **Bank account numbers** are stored as plain text in the database. Consider encrypting them before launch.
- **Sign-up screen:** add the line "By signing up you agree to the Terms of use and Privacy policy", with links.
