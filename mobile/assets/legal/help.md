# Help and support

Answers to common questions. Still stuck? Write to us at {{contactEmail}} and tell us your registered mobile number and what went wrong.

## Account

### How do I change my name, mobile number or photo?

Open Profile and choose Edit profile. Tap your photo to change it.

### I forgot my password

Write to {{contactEmail}} from the email address on your account and we will help you back in. If you are still signed in, you can set a new one in Profile, Change password.

### How do I delete my account?

Write to {{contactEmail}} from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it.

## Booking and renting

### Why can't I see a business or an item?

A business shows only after it is verified. A rent item shows only while it is available and has stock left for your dates. Also check your district filter.

### How does the advance work on a rent order?

An item may ask for an advance, either fixed for the whole order or an amount for each piece. The order shows the total, the advance and the balance. The balance is paid before the items are returned.

### How do I cancel?

Open the booking or rent order and choose Cancel. Any cancellation charge is shown before you confirm.

## Payments

### Which payments are made in the app?

Online payments go through Razorpay. A business can also take a payment by its UPI QR code or in cash, and mark it received on the order.

### I paid but the order still shows unpaid

Online payments usually show within a minute. For a UPI QR or cash payment, ask the business to mark it received. If it still looks wrong, write to us with the order details.

## For businesses

### How do I get verified?

Complete your business profile. We check it and verify the business. You will see the status on your profile.

### Where do customers pay me by UPI?

Add your UPI ID on your business profile. Your orders then show a QR code for the exact amount, which any UPI app can scan.

## For workers

### How do I get paid?

Add your UPI ID or bank account on your profile. The business pays you through the app.

## Permissions

### Why does the app ask for location, camera or notifications?

Location shows jobs and services near you, the camera takes photos and scans QR codes, and notifications tell you about your activity. Each is asked only when needed, and you can change them in App permissions.
