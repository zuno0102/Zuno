# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: {{lastUpdated}}**

{{appName}} ("we", "us") is run by {{companyName}}. This policy explains what we collect when you use the {{appName}} app and website, why we collect it, who can see it, and the choices you have. It covers all three kinds of account: customers, businesses and workers, in Service, Rent and Marketplace.

We collect only what the app needs to do its job. We do not sell your data, we do not show advertising from outside companies, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers. The only adverts in the app are the ones businesses post themselves on the Marketplace.

## 1. What we collect

### Your account

- Your name, email address and mobile number.
- Your password. We never store it as you typed it, only as a secure one-way hash.
- Your account type: customer, business or worker.
- A profile photo, if you add one.
- If you book or rent: your district (not your exact position), read from your phone's location
  when you allow it or picked by you, so we show you services and rent items near you. You can
  change it or set it to everywhere at any time.

### If you run a business

- Your business name, type, description, logo and address.
- Your GST number, if you give it.
- Your UPI ID, so customers can pay you by scanning your QR code.
- The services, rent items, prices, photos, hire posts and full-time job posts you create.
- Your Marketplace posts — listings, food offers and adverts — with their prices, photos,
  district and area, the details you fill in (such as a vehicle's year or a plot's size), and the
  contact number you give on an advert.
- Your Marketplace plan and how many posts it allows.
- Your verification status, ratings, reviews and level.

### If you work through the app

- Your experience, rate, availability, photo and the videos or pictures you add to your profile.
- Your location, while you allow it, so we can show you jobs near you.
- Your payout details: a UPI ID or a bank account number with its IFSC code, so you can be paid.
- Your ratings and reviews.

### Bookings, rent orders and payments

- What you book or rent, the dates, the address it goes to and the people involved.
- The messages you send to a business, worker or customer in the app.
- Payment records: the amount, the method (online, UPI QR or cash), the payment reference and the status.
- Card, net-banking and UPI app payments made online go through our payment partner, Razorpay. We never see or store your card number, CVV or bank login.

### Job applications

- The answers and files you send when you apply for a full-time job. A business may ask for a document such as an Aadhaar copy or a resume.

### From your phone

- **Location**, only if you allow it: to show nearby jobs and services, and to pin an address on the map.
- **Camera and photos**, only if you allow it: to take or choose a profile photo, add photos to a service, rent item or Marketplace post, scan a booking QR code, or scan a UPI QR code.
- **Files**: a PDF or picture you choose for a job application.
- **Notifications**, only if you allow it: to tell you about your bookings, orders, messages and applications.
- Your sign-in session is kept in your phone's secure storage. Your chosen search area and distance are kept on your phone.

### When you use our servers

- Like any online service, our servers record technical details of each request, such as your IP address, the time and the address requested. We use these only to keep the service running and secure.

## 2. Why we use it

- To create your account and sign you in.
- To show businesses, services, rent items and jobs, and to make and manage bookings, rent orders and applications.
- To let customers, businesses and workers contact each other about a booking or order.
- To take, record and refund payments, and to pay workers.
- To verify businesses and keep ratings and reviews honest.
- To send you notifications and messages about your activity.
- To prevent fraud and abuse and to keep the service secure.
- To meet our legal duties, such as keeping payment records.

## 3. Who can see your information

- **Other users, only as needed.** A business sees the name, mobile number and address of a customer who books or rents from it. A worker sees the details of a job they are assigned to. A business sees what an applicant sent with an application to its job.
- **Everyone using the app.** A business's name, logo, services, rent items, Marketplace posts (with an advert's contact number), area, verification status, ratings and reviews are public in the app. A review you write is shown with your name.
- **Service providers who work for us**, only for the job they do:
  - Razorpay, to process online payments.
  - Render, Neon and Vercel, which host our servers, database and website.
  - OpenStreetMap and Esri, which provide the map pictures. Your phone fetches the map squares it shows from them.
- **The law.** We share information when the law requires it, or to protect our users and our service from fraud or harm.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

## 4. How long we keep it

- We keep your account information while your account is open.
- When a business rejects a job application, the files sent with it are deleted at once. When a business deletes a job post, every applicant's files and answers for it are deleted too.
- We keep payment and booking records for as long as the law requires, even after an account is closed.
- When you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal information, except what we must keep by law.

## 5. Your choices and rights

- **See and correct your details:** use Edit profile in the app.
- **Permissions:** you can allow or stop location, camera, photos and notifications at any time in App permissions or your phone's settings. The app keeps working; only the feature that needs it stops.
- **Delete your account:** write to us at {{contactEmail}} from the email address on your account and we will delete it.
- **Questions or complaints:** contact our Grievance Officer below.

## 6. Keeping it safe

- Everything between the app and our servers is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
- Passwords are stored only as secure hashes.
- Only the people a record belongs to, and staff who need it to run the service, can reach it.
- No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and fix problems quickly.

## 7. Children

{{appName}} is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. Changes to this policy

When the app starts collecting something new, or uses information in a new way, we update this policy and change the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app.
